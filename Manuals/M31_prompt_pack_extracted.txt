Each prompt deliberately repeats the architectural baseline so it can be copied into a separate AI/coding session without losing critical context.
The supplied SQL schema remains the source of truth. Recommended cross-field rules are implemented at the application/service layer unless you explicitly decide to alter the database schema.
Where existing SIMS components already exist (staff_activity, master tables, audit framework, notification subsystem, import/export framework), integrate with them rather than creating duplicate infrastructure.
Architecture Baseline
Django app boundary: keep Module 31 inside the established staff-activity domain rather than making it a disconnected micro-application.
Persistence: staff_professional_membership is a one-to-one subtype of staff_activity, with staff_activity_id serving as both primary key and foreign key.
Business logic: cross-field and lifecycle rules belong in a service layer so API, forms, admin, imports, and scheduled jobs do not diverge.
MySQL: preserve InnoDB and utf8mb4 semantics; retain the supplied date check and composite unique constraint.
PWA: cache static assets and carefully selected read-only data only; never treat authenticated staff records as public/cache-forever content.
Security: enforce authorization server-side at query, permission, and service levels; do not rely on UI visibility.

Recommended Application-Level Invariants
Lifetime consistency: is_lifetime_membership=True implies membership_status=LIFETIME, membership_expiry_date=NULL, and renewal_required=False.
Reverse lifetime consistency: membership_status=LIFETIME implies is_lifetime_membership=True.
Manual administrative states (SUSPENDED, CANCELLED) should not be overwritten by an automatic expiry job.
An ACTIVE non-lifetime record with an expiry date before the effective date is eligible for explicit/automated transition to EXPIRED under policy.
Membership number uniqueness is scoped to organization; same number may be valid in different organizations.
Blank membership numbers should be normalized consistently to NULL before validation/persistence to avoid inconsistent uniqueness behavior.
Ownership/linkage to staff_activity must be determined and authorized server-side.
