1. Non-negotiable implementation rules
Preserve the existing SIMS modular-monolith architecture and custom user/RBAC approach.
Reuse existing employee, user_account, role/permission, state/country, document_type, file_object/object storage and general activity-audit facilities.
Never persist plaintext bank account, PAN or Aadhaar identifiers. Encrypt before persistence and create deterministic keyed HMAC fingerprints in trusted application code.
Normal UI/API responses expose masked/last-four values only. Do not leak raw identifiers in logs, errors, admin, exports, traces or audit payloads.
Evidence binaries remain in protected object storage; only file_object references and metadata are stored in this module.
Every decrypt/view/download/export of restricted information must be recorded in employee_sensitive_access_audit and the general activity audit.
Use transaction-safe services for multi-step changes. Do not silently weaken SQL constraints, ownership rules, verification or approval workflows.
